Poké Ball Grid

One ball per row, column, and zone.

How to play
  1. Put exactly one ball in every row, every column, and every color zone.
  2. Balls can't touch each other, even diagonally.
  3. Tap a square once for ✕ (a reminder), twice for a ball, three times to clear it.
  4. A wrong ball costs a heart. Lose all 3 and the puzzle restarts.
  5. Win by finishing fast. Every player's time goes on the high score list, fastest first.
